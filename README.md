# ISO 27001 Case Study
This is a retrospective analysis of a previous project where I identified and fixed an access-control weakness within a private SaaS membership programme while working as the Community Manager. The project originally began as a technical access-control problem. Having since developed my understanding of ISO 27001 and information security risk management, I have revisited the work from a governance perspective.

## Original Case Study
The original case study documents what I identified, how I approached the problem, the technical fix and the outcome:
https://chriscownden.com/case-studies/securing-a-members-only-community-using-a-compensating-control

### ISO 27001 Retrospective Report
This report builds on that work by examining the project through an ISO 27001 and information security risk management lens.

## Case Study Outline
### 1. Executive Summary
Overview of the original issue, response and outcome.

### 2. Business & Security Context
The SaaS environment, membership programme and relevant stakeholders. 

### 3. Identified Risk
The access-control weakness, affected assets and potential confidentiality, integrity and availability impacts.

### 4. Risk Assessment
Consideration of likelihood, impact and overall risk.

### 5. Risk Treatment
The options considered, chosen treatment and rationale.

### 6. Control & Governance
Relevant security controls, ownership, evidence and accountability.

### 7. Implementation & Validation
Changes made, testing performed and evidence of effectiveness.

### 8. Retrospective ISO 27001 Review
How my approach would differ today based on my understanding of ISO 27001 and risk management.

### 9. Lessons Learned
Key technical, governance and professional lessons from the project.

### 10. Conclusion
Summary of the security improvement and how the project demonstrates the ability to translate practical security work into governance-focused risk management.
